\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Fast feedback changes how people write.
\Citet{knuth1984} designed \TeX{} for batch typesetting, yet modern engines such as XeTeX \citep{kew2008} are quick enough for an interactive loop.
\Cref{sec:results} reports the latency we observed, and \cref{fig:loop} sketches the loop itself.
